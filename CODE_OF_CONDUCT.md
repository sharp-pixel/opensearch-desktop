# Code of Conduct

Everyone should be able to contribute to OpenSearch Developer Workbench without
harassment or discrimination. This policy applies to repository issues, pull
requests, reviews, project discussions, and interactions when representing the
project. It applies equally to contributors, users, and maintainers.

## Community standards

- Welcome people with different backgrounds, identities, abilities, and levels
  of experience.
- Discuss ideas and technical tradeoffs respectfully. Critique work rather than
  attacking the person who contributed it.
- Make feedback constructive, listen to corrections, and take responsibility for
  mistakes.
- Respect people's privacy, time, and personal boundaries.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexual harassment, and deliberately
disruptive behavior are unacceptable. Do not reveal someone else's private
information without permission. Retaliation against someone for raising a
good-faith concern is also unacceptable.

## Reporting

Send private reports to Cédric Pelvet at
[cedric.pelvet@gmail.com](mailto:cedric.pelvet@gmail.com). Include what happened,
relevant links or evidence, and how you would prefer to be contacted. Avoid
posting sensitive allegations or personal information in public issues.

Reports are handled by the [project maintainer](MAINTAINERS.md), with information
shared only as needed to investigate and respond. Do not assume absolute
confidentiality; discuss any specific privacy needs when reporting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will consider the available information, hear relevant
perspectives where appropriate, and explain the response to the people involved
when it is safe to do so. Responses may include requesting a correction, removing
content, issuing a warning, or restricting participation temporarily or
permanently. Serious behavior may warrant an immediate restriction.

You may request reconsideration through the same private contact. This project
currently has one maintainer and no independent appeals panel. If a concern
involves that maintainer or you cannot safely use the project contact, GitHub's
own reporting tools remain available for behavior on its platform.

These standards are informed by the
[OpenSearch Code of Conduct](https://opensearch.org/code-of-conduct/).
Enforcement for this independently maintained repository is the responsibility
of its maintainer; this policy does not establish Foundation oversight.
