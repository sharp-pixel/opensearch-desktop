## Responsibilities and decisions

Maintainers review pull requests, triage issues, maintain tests and dependencies,
coordinate releases, and handle the reporting processes in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
They also maintain contribution and licensing requirements.

Discuss substantial changes in repository issues and record the decision and
reasoning there. Maintainers seek agreement with contributors and make the final
scope and merge decisions. Security reports and sensitive conduct matters use
the private channels documented in their respective policies.

There is currently one maintainer. Additional reviewers are welcome; independent
review cannot yet be guaranteed for every maintainer-authored change. Maintenance
and support are provided on a best-effort basis.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who consistently help with implementation, reviews, documentation,
issue triage, and community support can propose becoming a maintainer in an issue
or pull request. Describe the responsibilities you can take on and link examples
of your work. Current maintainers discuss the proposal with the candidate and
record their decision publicly. Accepted appointments update this file and the
repository permissions.

Maintainers can step down by updating this file and arranging a handover of any
release or security responsibilities. Former maintainers may be listed as
emeritus with their agreement.

For the practices we aim to align with as the project develops, see
[OpenSearch maintainer responsibilities](https://github.com/opensearch-project/.github/blob/main/RESPONSIBILITIES.md).
